ABC RETAIL PVT. LTD.
EMPLOYEE POLICY

Document Code: EP-001
Department: Human Resources
Applicable Area: Employee and Department Management
Effective Period: January 2025 onwards

1. PURPOSE

The Employee Policy defines the basic organizational standards for maintaining employee information and managing the company's workforce.

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. maintains employee records containing:

* Employee ID
* Employee Name
* Department ID
* Job Role
* City
* Hire Date
* Salary

Employees are assigned to departments according to their responsibilities.

2. EMPLOYEE IDENTIFICATION

Every employee must have a unique employee ID.

The employee ID is the official identifier used by Human Resources for employee records.

Employee names should be recorded accurately.

Employee records must be maintained by authorized HR personnel.

3. DEPARTMENT STRUCTURE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. currently operates through the following departments:

Sales
Data & Analytics
Human Resources
Finance
Operations

Each department has a unique department ID.

The company department structure includes:

D01 – Sales
D02 – Data & Analytics
D03 – Human Resources
D04 – Finance
D05 – Operations

Each department has an assigned manager and location.

4. SALES DEPARTMENT

The Sales Department is responsible for customer acquisition, sales activities, customer relationships, and order-related business operations.

Employees in Sales may perform roles such as Sales Executive, Sales Representative, Sales Manager, or related positions.

Sales employees interact with customers and support the company's revenue-generating activities.

5. DATA & ANALYTICS DEPARTMENT

The Data & Analytics Department supports business reporting, data analysis, dashboards, business intelligence, and analytical activities.

Employees in this department may perform roles such as Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Scientist, BI Analyst, or related analytical positions.

The department supports management by providing reliable business information.

6. HUMAN RESOURCES DEPARTMENT

The Human Resources Department manages employee-related activities.

HR responsibilities may include:

* Employee records
* Recruitment
* Onboarding
* Employee policies
* Compensation administration
* Employee documentation
* Department assignments
* Workforce planning

Employee information must be handled confidentially.

7. FINANCE DEPARTMENT

The Finance Department manages financial activities and supports financial reporting.

Responsibilities may include:

* Financial reporting
* Budget monitoring
* Cost management
* Financial controls
* Business expense management
* Financial planning

Finance employees may work with business departments to monitor financial performance.

8. OPERATIONS DEPARTMENT

The Operations Department supports the company's day-to-day operational activities.

Responsibilities may include:

* Order fulfillment
* Operational coordination
* Process management
* Delivery coordination
* Business operations
* Service support

Operations employees coordinate with Sales and other departments when required.

9. EMPLOYEE JOB ROLES

Each employee is assigned a job role.

Job roles identify the primary responsibilities of employees.

Employees should perform duties according to their assigned roles and departmental responsibilities.

Job roles should be maintained accurately in employee records.

10. EMPLOYEE LOCATION

Employee records contain a city.

The employee city represents the location associated with the employee's company record.

Management may review employee distribution by city and department.

Location information may support workforce planning and office management.

11. HIRING

The Hire Date identifies when an employee joined ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd.

Human Resources maintains employee hiring records.

Management may review hiring activity by:

* Year
* Department
* Job role
* City

Hiring trends can help management understand workforce growth.

12. EMPLOYEE TENURE

Employee tenure represents the length of time an employee has been associated with the company.

Tenure may be evaluated using the employee's hire date.

Management may review workforce tenure across departments.

Tenure analysis can support workforce planning and organizational understanding.

13. SALARY MANAGEMENT

Every employee record contains a salary value.

Salary information is confidential company information.

Employee compensation should be managed according to company-approved compensation policies.

Salary information may be reviewed in aggregate for workforce planning and budget management.

Individual employee salary information should only be accessed by authorized personnel.

14. DEPARTMENT SALARY ANALYSIS

Management may review salary information at the department level.

Department-level analysis may include:

* Total salary expenditure
* Average salary
* Median salary
* Salary distribution
* Salary by job role
* Salary by city

Department salary analysis should support workforce and budget planning.

15. DEPARTMENT BUDGET

Each department has an annual budget.

The company currently maintains department budgets for:

Sales
Data & Analytics
Human Resources
Finance
Operations

Department budgets are used for organizational financial planning.

Management may compare aggregate employee salary expenditure with the annual departmental budget.

16. DEPARTMENT MANAGERS

Each department has a designated manager.

The current organizational structure includes:

D02 – Data & Analytics – Kavya Reddy
D03 – Human Resources – Suresh Iyer
D04 – Finance – Divya Menon
D05 – Operations – Manish Yadav

Department managers are responsible for coordinating activities within their respective departments.

17. EMPLOYEE DATA ACCURACY

Employee IDs must be unique.

Employee names must be recorded accurately.

Every employee should have a valid department ID.

Job roles must reflect the employee's assigned position.

City information should be maintained consistently.

Hire dates must represent valid dates.

Salary information must be maintained accurately.

18. EMPLOYEE CONFIDENTIALITY

Employee information must be handled responsibly.

Salary information, personal information, and employment records should only be accessed by authorized personnel.

Employees must not disclose confidential employee information without authorization.

19. WORKFORCE REPORTING

Human Resources may prepare periodic workforce reports.

Reports may include:

* Total employees
* Employees by department
* Employees by job role
* Employees by city
* Hiring activity
* Average salary
* Total salary expenditure
* Department budget utilization

Workforce reports should use current employee records.

20. DEPARTMENT PERFORMANCE SUPPORT

Every department contributes to the company's overall operations.

Sales supports revenue generation.

Data & Analytics supports business intelligence.

Human Resources supports workforce management.

Finance supports financial management.

Operations supports day-to-day business processes.

Employees should understand how their roles contribute to departmental objectives.

21. EMPLOYEE DEVELOPMENT

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. encourages employees to develop skills relevant to their responsibilities.

Departments may identify training requirements based on job roles and business needs.

Training programs may include technical skills, communication, management, analytical skills, customer service, and operational procedures.

22. MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLE

The employee database represents the company's organizational workforce.

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. expects employee information to remain accurate, confidential, and properly maintained.

Human Resources, department managers, and authorized management personnel are responsible for ensuring that workforce information supports effective organizational planning.

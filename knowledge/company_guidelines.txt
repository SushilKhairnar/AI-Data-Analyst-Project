ABC RETAIL PVT. LTD.
COMPANY BUSINESS GUIDELINES

Document Code: CBG-001
Department: Corporate Management
Applicable Area: General Business Operations
Effective Period: January 2025 onwards

1. COMPANY PURPOSE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. operates as a retail organization serving customers through a structured product and order management system.

The company maintains customer, product, order, employee, and department information to support day-to-day operations and management activities.

Every department is responsible for maintaining accurate information related to its area of responsibility.

2. ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. operates through five primary departments:

D01 – Sales
D02 – Data & Analytics
D03 – Human Resources
D04 – Finance
D05 – Operations

Each department has defined responsibilities and an assigned manager.

3. SALES

The Sales Department manages customer relationships, customer acquisition, order activities, sales operations, and sales performance monitoring.

Sales employees are responsible for ensuring that customer and order information is recorded correctly.

Sales activities should follow the company's approved sales and customer-management procedures.

4. DATA & ANALYTICS

The Data & Analytics Department supports business reporting and analytical activities.

The department works with information related to sales, customers, products, employees, and business performance.

Analytical reports should use consistent company definitions.

Business metrics should be clearly defined before they are used in management reports.

5. HUMAN RESOURCES

Human Resources manages employee information and workforce-related activities.

HR is responsible for maintaining accurate employee records and supporting recruitment, onboarding, compensation administration, workforce planning, and employee policies.

Employee information must be treated as confidential business information.

6. FINANCE

Finance manages financial reporting, budgeting, cost management, and financial controls.

The Finance Department coordinates with other departments when financial information is required for business operations.

Department budgets should be reviewed regularly.

7. OPERATIONS

Operations manages the company's operational processes.

The department works closely with Sales and Customer Service to support order fulfillment and customer requirements.

Operational processes should be monitored to identify delays and improve business efficiency.

8. CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Customer records contain:

* Customer ID
* Name
* City
* State
* Signup Date
* Customer Segment

Customer IDs must be unique.

Customer information should be maintained accurately.

Changes to customer information should be made only through approved business procedures.

9. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. uses the following customer segments:

Premium
Regular
Occasional
New
At Risk

Customer segmentation supports customer relationship management.

Employees should use the approved segment definitions when preparing customer reports.

10. PRODUCT INFORMATION

Product records contain:

* Product ID
* Product Name
* Category
* Price
* Cost

Product IDs must remain consistent across sales records.

Product names and categories should be maintained accurately.

Product prices and costs should be updated only through authorized procedures.

11. ORDER INFORMATION

Orders contain:

* Order ID
* Customer ID
* Order Date
* Status

Every order must be associated with a valid customer.

The approved order statuses are:

Delivered
Shipped
Pending
Cancelled
Refunded

Employees must select the status that accurately represents the order's current business state.

12. ORDER ITEMS

Order items identify the products purchased in an order.

Order item records contain:

* Order Item ID
* Order ID
* Product ID
* Quantity

Every order item must reference a valid order and product.

Quantity must be recorded accurately.

13. COMPLETED SALES

Delivered orders represent completed customer purchases under the company's sales reporting rules.

Pending and Shipped orders represent orders that have not yet been confirmed as completed.

Cancelled orders should be excluded from normal completed-sales reporting.

Refunded orders should be reviewed separately because they represent transactions that have entered the refund process.

14. CUSTOMER SERVICE

Customer Service is responsible for assisting customers with questions, order issues, cancellations, and refunds.

Customer Service should provide accurate information regarding order status.

Employees should not promise outcomes that have not been approved.

15. REFUND AND CANCELLATION

Cancellation and refund are separate business processes.

Cancelled orders represent orders that have been cancelled.

Refunded orders represent orders that have entered the company's refund process.

All refund requests must follow the applicable company procedure.

Finance should coordinate with Customer Service when financial processing is required.

16. EMPLOYEE INFORMATION

Employee records contain:

* Employee ID
* Employee Name
* Department ID
* Job Role
* City
* Hire Date
* Salary

Employee information must be maintained accurately.

Salary information is confidential.

17. DEPARTMENT MANAGEMENT

Each department has an annual budget and manager.

Department managers are responsible for coordinating their department's activities.

18. BUDGET MANAGEMENT

Department budgets should be monitored throughout the financial year.

Managers should understand their department's planned financial requirements.

Significant differences between planned budgets and actual expenditure should be reviewed by the responsible management and Finance teams.

19. DATA QUALITY

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. expects business records to be accurate and complete.

The following principles apply:

Customer IDs should be unique.

Product IDs should be unique.

Order IDs should be unique.

Order items should reference valid orders.

Order items should reference valid products.

Orders should reference valid customers.

Employee records should reference valid departments.

Dates should contain valid values.

Approved status and segment values should be used consistently.

20. BUSINESS REPORTING

Business reports should use approved company definitions.

Reports may cover:

* Sales
* Customers
* Products
* Orders
* Employees
* Departments
* Budgets
* Customer segments
* Product categories

Reports should clearly identify the reporting period.

21. MANAGEMENT DECISIONS

Management decisions should be based on accurate and relevant company information.

Managers should review multiple business indicators before making significant decisions.

For example, product performance should not be evaluated solely by sales volume when product cost and profitability information is available.

Customer performance should not be evaluated only by customer count.

Department performance should consider workforce, budget, and operational requirements.

22. CROSS-DEPARTMENT COORDINATION

Departments should coordinate when business processes overlap.

Sales and Operations should coordinate on order fulfillment.

Sales and Customer Service should coordinate on customer issues.

Customer Service and Finance should coordinate on approved refunds.

Human Resources and department managers should coordinate on workforce requirements.

Finance and department managers should coordinate on budgets.

Data & Analytics should coordinate with business departments to ensure reports correctly represent business definitions.

23. CONFIDENTIALITY

Company information must be handled responsibly.

Customer information should only be accessed for legitimate business purposes.

Employee salary and employment information should only be accessed by authorized personnel.

Business information should not be disclosed outside approved company processes.

24. BUSINESS PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. encourages departments to identify opportunities for improving business processes.

Improvement activities may focus on:

* Faster order processing
* Better customer service
* Accurate product information
* Improved sales performance
* Reduced cancellations
* Better refund management
* Efficient workforce planning
* Better budget management
* Improved reporting

25. OFFICIAL BUSINESS DEFINITIONS

The company should maintain consistent definitions for important business terms.

For example:

Completed Order means a Delivered order.

Cancelled Order means an order with Cancelled status.

Refunded Order means an order with Refunded status.

Customer Segment refers to the approved customer classifications.

Department Budget refers to the approved annual budget assigned to a department.

26. MANAGEMENT REVIEW

Management should periodically review company performance across sales, customers, products, employees, and departments.

Management reviews may include:

* Sales performance
* Customer growth
* Customer segment distribution
* Product performance
* Order status distribution
* Cancellation activity
* Refund activity
* Employee headcount
* Salary expenditure
* Department budgets

27. CORE COMPANY PRINCIPLE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. aims to maintain a structured, customer-focused, and data-driven business environment.

Every department has a defined responsibility.

Every customer should receive appropriate service.

Every order should be recorded accurately.

Every product should have reliable information.

Every employee should have an accurate organizational record.

Every department should manage its resources responsibly.

Accurate information, clear business processes, responsible management, and cooperation between departments are fundamental principles of ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd.

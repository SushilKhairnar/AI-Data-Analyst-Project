ABC RETAIL PVT. LTD.
SALES POLICY

Document Code: SP-001
Department: Sales
Applicable Area: Sales Operations and Order Management
Effective Period: January 2025 onwards

1. PURPOSE

The Sales Policy of ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. establishes the standard procedures for managing customer orders, monitoring sales activities, handling order statuses, and maintaining consistent sales reporting across the organization.

The company operates through a structured customer and product sales process. Customers are registered in the company's customer master and are identified using a unique customer ID. Products available for sale are maintained in the product catalog using unique product IDs. Orders connect customers with the products they purchase.

All sales activities must follow the procedures described in this policy.

2. CUSTOMER AND ORDER IDENTIFICATION

Every customer registered with ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. receives a unique customer ID. Customer records contain the customer's name, city, state, signup date, and customer segment.

A customer must have a valid customer record before an order can be created.

Every sales order receives a unique order ID. The order record identifies the customer who placed the order, the date on which the order was created, and the current order status.

Sales employees must ensure that customer and order information is entered accurately.

3. ORDER PROCESSING

A standard customer order consists of an order record and one or more product items associated with that order.

The order record identifies the customer and the order date. Individual purchased products are recorded through order items. Each order item identifies the product and the quantity purchased.

The product catalog must be used when selecting products for an order. Product IDs must correspond to valid products maintained by the company.

The quantity recorded for an order item must represent the number of units purchased.

Sales personnel must verify customer information, product information, and quantity before completing order entry.

4. ORDER STATUS

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. uses the following order statuses:

Delivered
Shipped
Pending
Cancelled
Refunded

Delivered indicates that the order has successfully reached the customer.

Shipped indicates that the order has been dispatched and is in the delivery process.

Pending indicates that the order is still awaiting further processing.

Cancelled indicates that the order has been cancelled and will not proceed through normal fulfillment.

Refunded indicates that the order has been processed as a refund according to applicable company procedures.

Employees must update order status accurately and avoid assigning a status that does not represent the actual stage of the order.

5. SALES COMPLETION

For company sales reporting, a delivered order represents a completed customer purchase.

Shipped orders represent orders that have left the company's fulfillment process but have not yet been confirmed as delivered.

Pending orders should be monitored by the Sales and Operations teams because they require additional processing.

Cancelled orders should be reviewed to understand why customers did not complete their purchases.

Refunded orders should be monitored separately because the original purchase has subsequently entered the refund process.

6. SALES BY PRODUCT

The Sales Department reviews product-level sales performance regularly.

Product analysis includes:

* Product name
* Product category
* Number of units sold
* Number of orders containing the product
* Sales value
* Customer demand
* Product contribution to category sales

Product information is maintained in the company's product catalog.

Products have a product ID, product name, category, price, and cost.

Sales teams should monitor products with strong customer demand as well as products showing declining activity.

7. SALES BY CATEGORY

ABC Retail organizes products into categories. Category-level sales reporting is used to understand customer demand across different product groups.

Managers should review:

* Total sales by category
* Units sold by category
* Number of orders by category
* Average product price
* Product cost
* Profit contribution
* Customer demand

Category performance should be reviewed regularly so that management can identify areas of strong demand and areas requiring additional attention.

8. SALES BY CUSTOMER SEGMENT

Customers are classified into business segments including:

* Premium
* Regular
* Occasional
* New
* At Risk

Sales teams should monitor purchasing activity across these segments.

Premium customers represent an important customer group and should receive appropriate attention from customer service and sales teams.

New customers should be monitored during their initial relationship with the company.

At Risk customers should be reviewed for declining purchasing activity.

Segment-level sales information should be used to understand differences in customer purchasing behavior.

9. GEOGRAPHICAL SALES

Customer location information includes city and state.

Sales management may review sales performance by city and state to understand geographic demand.

Regional sales reports should identify areas where customer activity is strong and areas where sales activity requires improvement.

The customer location recorded in the customer master should be used as the official customer location for company reporting.

10. SALES BY TIME PERIOD

Sales performance should be monitored across daily, monthly, quarterly, and yearly periods.

Order dates are used to determine when customer orders were placed.

Monthly sales reviews should compare sales activity across months and identify changes in customer demand.

Management may review:

* Monthly order volume
* Monthly completed sales
* Monthly product demand
* Monthly category performance
* Monthly customer-segment performance
* Monthly cancellation activity
* Monthly refund activity

Year-over-year comparisons may also be performed when sufficient historical information is available.

11. SALES PERFORMANCE

The Sales Department is responsible for monitoring sales activity and identifying changes in business performance.

Important sales indicators include:

* Total completed sales
* Number of completed orders
* Units sold
* Average order value
* Sales by category
* Sales by customer segment
* Sales by geographic area
* Cancellation activity
* Refund activity

Sales managers should review these indicators regularly.

12. ORDER CANCELLATION

Cancelled orders must be recorded using the Cancelled status.

Sales employees should review cancellation activity to identify recurring problems.

Potential areas for investigation include:

* Product availability
* Customer requirements
* Order processing delays
* Pricing concerns
* Customer service issues
* Delivery expectations

The company should maintain accurate cancellation records to support management reviews.

13. REFUNDED ORDERS

Refunded orders must be identified separately from completed delivered orders.

A refunded order should not be treated as a normal successfully completed sale when management reviews final business performance.

Refund processing must follow the company's refund policy and applicable approval procedures.

Sales and Finance teams should coordinate when refund-related financial information is reviewed.

14. SALES RESPONSIBILITIES

The Sales Department is responsible for:

* Customer acquisition
* Customer relationship management
* Order creation
* Sales monitoring
* Sales reporting
* Customer segment review
* Product sales monitoring
* Regional sales analysis
* Cancellation monitoring
* Coordination with customer service

Sales employees must maintain accurate customer and order information.

15. PRODUCT PRICING

Products maintained by ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. have a standard selling price and cost.

The price represents the standard product selling price maintained in the product catalog.

The cost represents the company's recorded product cost.

Any approved price change must be reflected in the official product catalog.

Sales employees must use current approved product pricing when processing customer orders.

16. SALES DATA QUALITY

Sales records must contain valid identifiers.

Customer IDs used in orders must correspond to registered customers.

Product IDs used in order items must correspond to products in the product catalog.

Order IDs must be unique.

Order dates must represent valid business dates.

Order statuses must use the company's approved status values.

Incorrect or incomplete records should be reported to the appropriate business team for correction.

17. SALES REVIEW PROCESS

The Sales Manager should conduct periodic reviews of sales performance.

Monthly reviews should examine order activity, customer activity, product performance, category performance, and order status distribution.

Significant changes in sales activity should be investigated before management decisions are made.

Sales reports should use consistent company definitions so that performance can be compared across reporting periods.

18. MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. considers accurate sales information essential for effective business operations.

Every order should have a valid customer, every order item should refer to a valid product, and every order status should accurately represent the customer's order journey.

The Sales Department should focus not only on increasing sales but also on maintaining accurate customer relationships, reliable order processing, and consistent sales operations.

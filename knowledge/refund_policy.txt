ABC RETAIL PVT. LTD.
REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Document Code: RCP-001
Department: Customer Service and Finance
Applicable Area: Order Cancellation and Refund Management
Effective Period: January 2025 onwards

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the company's procedures for handling customer order cancellations and refunds.

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. records order status to identify the current stage of an order.

The approved order statuses are:

* Delivered
* Shipped
* Pending
* Cancelled
* Refunded

Cancellation and refund activities must be recorded accurately.

2. ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers may request cancellation of an order according to the applicable stage of order processing.

Cancellation requests should be reviewed by the responsible Customer Service or Sales team.

When an order is approved for cancellation, the order status should be updated to Cancelled.

Cancelled orders should remain identifiable in company records for business review and operational analysis.

3. PENDING ORDERS

A Pending order has not yet completed the normal fulfillment process.

Pending orders should be monitored by Sales and Operations teams.

Customer Service may contact the customer when additional information or action is required.

Pending orders should not automatically be treated as cancelled or refunded.

4. SHIPPED ORDERS

A Shipped order has been dispatched for delivery.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may require additional review because the order has entered the delivery process.

The Customer Service team should follow applicable operational procedures when a cancellation request is received for a shipped order.

5. DELIVERED ORDERS

A Delivered order represents an order that has reached the customer.

Delivered orders are considered completed purchases for normal sales operations.

If a customer requests a refund after delivery, the request should be evaluated according to the company's refund procedure.

6. REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Refund requests should be reviewed before approval.

The company may consider:

* Order status
* Product involved
* Customer request
* Applicable return conditions
* Order processing stage
* Customer service information
* Business approval requirements

Not every cancellation or customer complaint automatically results in a refund.

7. REFUND PROCESS

The standard refund process consists of:

1. Customer submits a refund request.
2. Customer Service reviews the request.
3. Order details are verified.
4. Eligibility is assessed.
5. Approval is obtained when required.
6. The order is recorded as Refunded when the refund is approved and processed.
7. Finance completes applicable financial processing.

The company should maintain accurate status information throughout the process.

8. REFUNDED STATUS

The Refunded status identifies orders that have entered the company's refund process.

Refunded orders should be monitored separately from normal completed orders.

Management should review refund activity regularly to understand customer service and product-related trends.

9. CANCELLATION VS REFUND

Cancellation and refund are different business events.

Cancellation occurs when an order is stopped before normal completion.

Refund occurs when money associated with a purchase is returned according to the company's approved refund process.

An order should not be classified as Refunded simply because a customer requested cancellation.

The actual approved business event must determine the order status.

10. CUSTOMER SERVICE RESPONSIBILITIES

Customer Service is responsible for receiving and reviewing customer requests.

Customer Service may need to verify:

* Customer identity
* Order ID
* Order date
* Product purchased
* Order status
* Reason for the request
* Applicable company conditions

Customer Service should provide customers with clear information regarding the status of their request.

11. SALES RESPONSIBILITIES

The Sales Department should support customer communication when cancellation or refund requests relate to sales activity.

Sales employees should not independently approve refunds unless they have the required authority.

Sales teams should coordinate with Customer Service and Finance when necessary.

12. FINANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

The Finance Department is responsible for the financial processing associated with approved refunds.

Finance should maintain appropriate records of refund transactions according to company financial procedures.

Refund information should be reconciled with approved customer service records.

13. REFUND MONITORING

Management should monitor refund activity periodically.

Useful business measures include:

* Number of refunded orders
* Refund rate
* Refunds by month
* Refunds by product
* Refunds by category
* Refunds by customer segment
* Refunds by location

Refund activity should be compared with total order activity to understand its relative level.

14. CANCELLATION MONITORING

Cancelled orders should also be monitored.

Management may review cancellation activity by:

* Month
* Customer
* Product
* Category
* Customer segment
* City
* State

A high level of cancellations may require additional investigation into customer experience, product expectations, pricing, or operational processes.

15. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS

Refund and cancellation activity may vary across customer segments.

The company may review activity among:

* Premium
* Regular
* Occasional
* New
* At Risk

Customer segment information should be used to understand patterns rather than automatically assuming the reason for a cancellation or refund.

16. PRODUCT ANALYSIS

The company may review refunds and cancellations by product.

Product-level analysis can identify whether particular products experience unusually high levels of customer dissatisfaction or cancellation.

Management should investigate relevant business factors before taking action.

17. CATEGORY ANALYSIS

Category-level refund analysis provides a broader view than individual product analysis.

Management may compare categories according to order volume and refund or cancellation activity.

Categories with high transaction volume may naturally have more refund events, so activity should be considered in relation to total orders.

18. REFUND DATA ACCURACY

Order IDs must be valid.

Customer IDs associated with orders must be valid.

Order statuses must use approved values.

Refunded orders must be correctly identified.

Cancelled orders must not be incorrectly marked as Refunded.

Employees should correct inaccurate status information through approved company procedures.

19. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

Customers must receive clear and professional communication regarding cancellation and refund requests.

Customer Service should explain whether the request is:

* Under review
* Approved
* Rejected
* Cancelled
* Refunded

Employees should not promise a refund before the appropriate approval has been completed.

20. RECORD RETENTION

Cancellation and refund records should be retained according to company record-management procedures.

Historical information may be required for customer service reviews, financial reconciliation, operational analysis, and management reporting.

21. MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLE

ABC Retail Pvt. Ltd. treats cancellation and refund management as an important part of customer service and operational control.

Every request should be reviewed according to the applicable business process.

Order status must accurately represent the actual state of the customer transaction.

Clear communication, proper approval, accurate records, and coordination between Customer Service, Sales, Operations, and Finance are essential for effective refund and cancellation management.
